A chapter I will never forget
When I was scared for you

Dad,

When you had to undergo heart surgery, everything suddenly felt different. You were the person I had always seen as strong, protective, and somehow able to handle anything — and suddenly, I was the one hoping with all my heart that you would be okay.

I was scared in a way that is difficult to put into words. I kept thinking about how much you mean to me and how much of my life has you in it. In those moments, I realized just how deeply I depend on your presence, your voice, your advice, your jokes, and simply knowing that my dad is there.

“I just wanted my dad to be okay.”

I wished I could take away your fear and carry the difficult part for you. I would have done anything to make things easier. All I could do was hope, pray, and wait for the moment I could know that you were okay.

That experience changed the way I look at time with you. It reminded me not to take the little things for granted: conversations, laughs, family moments, even the ordinary days. Those are the moments I want more of with you.

I am so grateful that you made it through that difficult chapter. And I want you to know that your daughter Malak loves you more deeply than she probably knows how to say.

I need you here for a very long time. ♡

What I really want you to know

Dad,

I don't think I say it enough, but I am so grateful that I get to call you my dad. I am so lucky that I get to be your daughter, and I hope you always know how much that means to me.

You have been there through every version of me — the tiny girl, the dramatic girl, the stubborn girl, and the girl who is still figuring life out. Through all of them, you have loved me, protected me, and made me feel like I never have to face life alone.

Then came the time you had to undergo heart surgery. I don't think I can fully explain how scared I felt. Seeing someone as important to me as you face something so serious made me realize, more than ever, just how much you mean to me.

I remember worrying, praying, and wishing I could somehow take away the fear and pain for you. I wanted nothing more than for my dad to be okay. In those moments, I realized how much of my heart is tied to yours and how much I need you in my life.

That experience taught me not to take our ordinary moments for granted. The conversations, the laughs, the little things, and simply knowing that you are there — those are the things I want more of. I want so many more memories with you, Hany.

I may grow older, but there will always be a part of me that is your little girl. And no matter where life takes me, I hope you always know how deeply loved you are.

You are not just my dad. You are my protector, one of my safest places, and one of the biggest pieces of my heart. I am endlessly grateful that you made it through that difficult chapter and that I get to keep calling you Dad.

Happy birthday to my dad, my Hany, my protector, my partner in crime, and one of the most important people in my life. I love you more than I could ever fit into a website, a letter, or a thousand birthday messages.

Love, Malak — your only girl ♡
